King Wa Tam

PhD · Epidemiology & Health EconomicsOpen to research & data roles

A PhD-trained researcher with over a decade of expertise in epidemiology, statistics, health economics, and quantitative social science, including over two years of postdoctoral fellowship at the Chinese University of Hong Kong and the University of Calgary. My work has taken me from Australia to Hong Kong to Canada, and as a consultant to the World Bank.

My work spans study design, statistical modelling, manuscript drafting, peer-review revision, and translating quantitative findings for diverse stakeholders, resulting in twelve peer-reviewed publications in journals such as PLOS Medicine, Cancers, BMC Geriatrics, and JMIR Public Health and Surveillance.

Alongside my research, I also have hands-on experience with independent agentic AI projects by leveraging rapid prototyping, automated testing, and software design to improve the efficiency, reproducibility, reliability, and scalability of my workflows.
